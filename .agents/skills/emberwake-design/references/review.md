# Review

## QC-001 — Compare against a named baseline

Identify the requirement or selected reference, target build or asset, and conditions tested. For a finding, give the affected rule or region, the visible or reproducible symptom, the expected result, and the supporting frame or steps. Separate an observed failure from a hypothesized cause.

## QC-002 — Match proof to the claim

For rules and balance, capture the encounter, inputs, and resulting event sequence, with timing when it affects the outcome. For visuals, inspect the actual rendered artifact at its intended size and compare the affected view, crop or motion frame. A passing export or coverage check supports a mechanical claim. Keep visual quality and user acceptance as separate results. With only a screenshot, bound the finding to the visible frame.

## QC-003 — Recheck the failure and nearby behavior

Repeat the failing case under comparable conditions and inspect the closest affected behavior. Report what passed, what still fails, and what remains unverified. When a verified correction yields a reusable prevention step, follow LEARN-001 in the skill entrypoint.
