# Game design

## GAME-001 — Start with a player decision

Describe what the player sees, the alternatives they can reasonably choose, and what each costs or risks. Then propose the rule and a scene where those choices produce different outcomes. For progression, distinguish the unlock condition from what it enables and what earlier choices remain useful.

## GAME-002 — Keep presentation and combat state distinct

In real-time combat, show how the player reads enemy wind-up, attack reach, and recovery while moving. A camera or effect change can affect what the player sees and when they react. Treat changes to movement, hit timing, reach, or interrupt windows as explicit design proposals.

## GAME-003 — Specify one playable slice

Choose the smallest encounter or loop that makes the proposed decision visible. State its initial conditions, normal outcome, failure or retreat outcome when applicable, and the observation that would justify iterating. Keep intentionally open lore or systems as open questions in the design.
